Materials

What our fragrances are made of

Around forty raw materials pass through our atelier. Some are grown in fields we have visited, others are built molecule by molecule in the lab. Here are the ones that matter most.

How a fragrance unfolds

Top, heart and base

A perfume isn’t one smell but a sequence. Lighter materials evaporate first and heavier ones last, so a fragrance changes on your skin over the course of a day.

  1. Top notes First 15 minutes

    Bright, volatile materials such as citrus, light spices and aldehydes. They make the first impression, then lift away.

  2. Heart notes 15 minutes – 4 hours

    Florals, greens and soft spices emerge as the top fades. This is the character of the fragrance, and what most people remember.

  3. Base notes 4 hours and beyond

    Woods, resins, musks and vanilla evaporate slowly, anchoring everything and lingering on skin into the next day.

Our promise

How we choose what goes in

  • i.

    Traceable naturals

    We buy from growers and distillers we know by name, and we publish the origin of every key material on this page.

  • ii.

    Responsible synthetics

    Lab-made molecules let us replace animal and endangered materials, like animal musk, ambergris and wild oud.

  • iii.

    Safe on skin

    Every formula is checked against the current IFRA standards before a single bottle is filled.

  • iv.

    Vegan & cruelty-free

    No animal-derived ingredients, and nothing is ever tested on animals.
