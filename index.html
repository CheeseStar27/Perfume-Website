sillage

/si·jaʒ/ · noun, French

The trail a fragrance leaves in the air — the part of you that stays in the room after you have gone.

Our house

Perfume, made the slow way.

We named our house after the most elusive quality in perfumery, and every fragrance we make is built to earn it: composed by hand from traceable materials, rested for six weeks before bottling, and poured into glass you can refill for years.
