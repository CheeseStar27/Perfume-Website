About Us

We make perfume slowly, on purpose.

Sillage is a small, independent perfume house. We compose, macerate and bottle everything ourselves, in batches small enough to number by hand.

Our story

It started with a blotter and a question.

Sillage began in 2019 at a kitchen table covered in glass vials and paper blotters. We wanted a perfume that smelled like it had been made by people rather than by a marketing brief. We couldn’t find one, so we started making our own.

The kitchen table is an atelier now, but the approach hasn’t changed. We source materials from growers we know, compose every formula by hand and let each batch rest for six weeks before it is filtered and bottled. Nothing is rushed, and nothing is made in quantities we can’t stand behind.

Our name is the French word for the trail a fragrance leaves behind. It is the quality we chase in every formula: not loudness, but presence.

What we believe

Four rules we don’t break

  • 01

    Small batches

    Every fragrance is blended in runs of 300 bottles or fewer, and each bottle is numbered by hand.

  • 02

    Time to rest

    Each batch macerates for six weeks so the materials can marry before it is chilled, filtered and bottled.

  • 03

    Refill, don’t replace

    Our heavy glass flacons are made to last for years. Refills cost 25% less and arrive in recyclable aluminium.

  • 04

    Kind by default

    Vegan, cruelty-free and checked against IFRA standards: every formula, every batch.

How it’s made

From field to flacon

Five steps, none of them skipped. It takes about three months to turn a finished formula into bottles on the shelf.

  1. Source

    Materials from growers and distillers we know by name, checked batch by batch.

  2. Compose

    Formulas weighed out by hand, gram by gram. Some take a year to get right.

  3. Macerate

    Six weeks of rest in steel tanks, away from light and heat, so the materials can marry.

  4. Bottle

    Chilled, filtered and poured into numbered, refillable glass flacons.

  5. Refill

    Bring your flacon back and we’ll fill it again for 25% less.
